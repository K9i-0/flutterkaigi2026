# WAI English narration direction

## Voice

- Professional adult male narrator.
- Calm mid-low register, precise diction, restrained confidence.
- Serious premium technology-launch delivery throughout.
- No irony, wink, exaggerated comedy, hesitation, or laughter.

## Required pronunciation

- Every written `WAI` is pronounced as Japanese katakana `ワ・イ`.
- Use two short morae: `wah-ee`, IPA approximately `[wa.i]`.
- Insert a very small separation between `wa` and `i`; give the second mora a slight emphasis.
- The pronunciation should sound like a fluent English narrator carefully but slightly awkwardly pronouncing a Japanese word.
- Never pronounce `WAI` as English `why` /waɪ/, `way` /weɪ/, or the spelled-out letters `double-u ay eye`.
- Read `Window-Side AI` and every other English phrase fluently and professionally.
