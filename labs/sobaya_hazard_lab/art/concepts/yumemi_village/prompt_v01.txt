A traditional fishing settlement with tiled roofs, timber siding, plaster, concrete seawalls, narrow drainage gutters, utility poles, terraced slopes and wooded hills. Corporate investment added a modest municipal building, prefab offices, concrete employee dormitory blocks and a research facility cut into the hillside. Company withdrawal left shuttered shops, abandoned housing, faded corporate-funded infrastructure. The island is still used as an exile posting for unwanted office employees; an absurdly maintained reception sign and a few fluorescent lights continue functioning. Not a bombed city or medieval European village. The tragedy is ordinary Japanese life abandoned by an employer. Geography evocative of islands around Shikoku but do not specify an actual location or draw a map.
